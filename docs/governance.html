Review expectations

The named owner checks factual accuracy and links. A subject-matter reviewer verifies affected technical steps. The content maintainer checks consistency, audience and readability. Capture a meaningful change note when publishing an update.

Lifecycle

Draft articles stay out of the live collection. Current articles have a named owner and a future review date. An article becomes review due when its date precedes the collection’s as-of date. A retired article retains its ID and replacement link where one exists.

Review triggers

Review after a product or process change, a repeated support issue, a broken instruction, or the scheduled date. A scheduled review date alone does not prove that the procedure is accurate.

Archiving

Document why an article is retired. Update inbound links and redirects where supported. Keep the last approved revision and record the replacement. Confirm retention requirements with the organization before deleting source records.
